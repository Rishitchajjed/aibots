Frequently Asked Questions

How can I create a free custom QR code?

Enter your website URL, text, email, Wi-Fi credentials, or UPI ID. Customize the foreground and background colors, adjust error correction levels, and click 'Download QR Code' as high-resolution PNG or SVG.

Do these generated QR codes ever expire?

No, never! These are static, permanent QR codes encoded directly with your data. They have zero scan limits and will continue working forever.

Can I generate UPI QR codes for payments?

Yes, select the UPI payment type, enter your VPA / UPI ID (e.g. yourname@upi) and optional payee name. Customers can scan your QR code with GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM to pay instantly.
