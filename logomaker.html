Are the logos free for commercial and business use?

Yes! All logos created on AI Bots are 100% free with full commercial rights. You can use them on your YouTube channel, Twitch stream, startup website, mobile app, merchandise, and business cards without attribution.

How do I create a transparent PNG logo?

Navigate to the Layout Tab and check "Transparent PNG Export". This removes the solid badge background and downloads only your icon and text cutout.
