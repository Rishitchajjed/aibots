Frequently Asked Questions

How do I crop a photo into a circle or square?

Upload your photo and choose from preset aspect ratios: 1:1 Square (Instagram/Avatar), Circular Crop, 16:9 Landscape, 4:3, 3:4 Portrait, or Freeform. Adjust the drag handles and click 'Crop & Download'.

Does cropping reduce the quality of my photo?

No, the cropper extracts the selected pixels directly from the source image at native resolution without blurring or degradation.

Can I rotate and flip my image before cropping?

Yes! Use the 90° Rotate Left/Right and Horizontal/Vertical Flip buttons to orient your photo before downloading.
