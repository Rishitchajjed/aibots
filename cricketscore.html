Frequently Asked Questions

How does this Cricket Score Counter work?

You can set up custom team names, total overs, and player names. During the match, tap buttons for runs (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 0), wickets, wides, no-balls, and byes. The tool updates the scorecard, current run rate (CRR), required run rate (RRR), and ball-by-ball commentary in real time.

Can I use this cricket scorer offline without an internet connection?

Yes! The Cricket Score Counter is an offline-capable Progressive Web App (PWA). Once loaded, all calculations, match states, and wagon-wheel data are stored locally in your browser.

Can I download or share the match scorecard?

Yes, you can export the full match summary and scorecard as a clean image or text report to share directly on WhatsApp, Instagram, or print out for tournaments.

Does it support custom tournament overs and player stats?

Yes, you can configure 5, 10, 15, 20 (T20), or any custom over count, track individual batsmen runs/balls, bowler figures (overs, maidens, runs, wickets), and match extras.
