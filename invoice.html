Frequently Asked Questions & Guide

How does the Dynamic UPI Payment QR Code work?

When you enter your UPI ID, AI Bots creates a real-time banking QR code containing both your UPI ID and the calculated Grand Total. Your client can scan the QR code from their mobile banking app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) to immediately pay the exact amount.

Are the downloaded invoices GST-compliant?

Yes! You can add HSN/SAC codes, company GSTIN, client GSTIN, and choose between single GST (18%) or split CGST (9%) + SGST (9%) with complete automatic tax calculations.

Is my business data and invoice saved securely?

Yes! All your profile data and drafts are stored locally on your device's browser (LocalStorage). Nothing is sent to an external server, ensuring 100% privacy and confidentiality.
