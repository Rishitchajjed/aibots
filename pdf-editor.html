Frequently Asked Questions

Can I edit existing text inside a PDF without converting to Word?
Yes! Click on the Edit Words tool and click or double-click any word in the document. You can edit the text directly inline, and the editor will automatically cover the original text and render your updated text matching the exact position and font size.
Is my sensitive data safe when editing financial or identity documents?
Yes, 100%. This tool operates completely inside your browser using WebAssembly and PDF-Lib. No file, text, signature, or image is ever uploaded to a server or third-party cloud.
How do I sign a PDF document online?
Click the Sign button in the top toolbar. You can draw your signature using your mouse/touchscreen, type your name to generate a cursive signature, or upload a photo of your signature. Once placed, you can resize, move, and position it anywhere on the page.
