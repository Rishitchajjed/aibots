How does the AI Background Remover work?

Our tool analyzes the visual contours, colors, and edges of your subject (people, products, animals, cars) and strips away the backdrop in seconds, providing a transparent PNG cutout ready for graphic design, eCommerce listings, or social media.

Can I change the backdrop to white for passport photos?

Yes! Inside the Backdrop Studio palette, simply click the White swatch or enter any passport color code to generate an official document-ready photo instantly.
