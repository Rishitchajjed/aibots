Frequently Asked Questions

Will this really vibrate my Apple Watch?
Yes! When you add this page to your iPhone Home Screen and enable notifications, iOS treats it as an installed web app. By default, iOS automatically forwards notifications to your paired Apple Watch whenever your iPhone screen is locked, vibrating your wrist with Apple’s native haptics.
How is this different from the "Merge" app?
The Merge app costs a monthly/yearly subscription and tries to maintain a continuous Bluetooth LE connection between Android and watchOS (which watchOS frequently suspends). This bridge tool is 100% free forever, has no subscriptions, and uses Apple's native iOS notification system which never gets killed in the background.
Is my notification data private?
Yes. Your Channel ID is randomly generated and unguessable (like a secret password). Only devices that know your exact channel key can subscribe or send alerts. No account, email, or personal data is collected or stored.
Does this require the iPhone to be turned on?
Yes, your iPhone 15 needs to be turned on and connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data (it can be kept at home or in your pocket/bag). Your Apple Watch connects through the iPhone to deliver the alerts to your wrist.
