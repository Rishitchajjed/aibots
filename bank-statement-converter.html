How do I fix "Referenced master is missing" in TallyPrime?

In TallyPrime, either: (1) On the Exceptions screen, press Alt + M (Map Master) or Enter to map the ledger to your existing ledger (e.g. Suspense A/c), OR (2) Click Download Masters XML and import it first via Alt + O > Masters.

Is my banking data uploaded to the internet?

No. Our converter runs 100% offline inside your browser using WebAssembly and PDF.js. Your financial details never leave your computer.

What if my bank statement has a PDF password?

The converter prompts you for your statement password (e.g. DOB DDMMYYYY or PAN) and unlocks it in-memory. We never store or log your password.

Does it work with Tally.ERP 9?

Yes! The generated XML uses the standard Tally XML envelope structure compatible with both Tally.ERP 9 and TallyPrime.

How to Import in Tally

Importing with Repotic TDL (Repotic.tcp):

  1. Ensure Repotic.tcp is loaded (F1: Help > TDLs & AddOns > F4: Manage Local TDLs).
  2. Press Esc to return to Gateway of Tally.
  3. Click on the Repotic menu option (or press R).
  4. Select Import Bank Statement.
  5. Select your Bank Ledger (e.g. Punjab National Bank).
  6. Choose either the Tally XML or Excel / CSV file downloaded from this tool and press Enter to import all transactions!

For Native TallyPrime (Without TDL):

  1. Open your company in TallyPrime.
  2. Step 1 (If ledgers are new): Click Download Masters XML > Press Alt + O (Import) > Select Masters > Choose the Masters XML file.
  3. Step 2 (Vouchers): Click Download Tally XML > Press Alt + O > Select Transactions > Choose file format XML > Select file and press Enter.

If you see "Referenced master is missing":

TallyPrime gives you two easy options:
Option A: On that exact "Import Exceptions" screen in TallyPrime, highlight the missing ledger and press Alt + M (Map Master) or Enter to map it to your existing ledger (e.g. Suspense A/c).
Option B: Use the "Batch Set Ledger" box on this page to set all rows to your existing ledger (e.g. Suspense A/c) and re-download.

For Tally.ERP 9:

  1. Gateway of Tally > Import of Data > Vouchers.
  2. Enter the full path and filename of the downloaded XML file.
  3. Press Enter to import.
