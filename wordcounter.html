Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this word counter?

It uses regex-powered tokenization to count words, characters with and without spaces, sentences, paragraphs, syllables, and estimated reading/speaking time in real time as you type.

Can it check reading time and keyword density?

Yes, it displays estimated reading time (based on 225 wpm) and speaking time, along with character limits for social media (Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram captions).

Is my text saved or sent to any server?

No. All text analysis runs entirely in your local browser memory. Nothing is sent across the network.
