Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 300% Audio Volume Booster work?

The Volume Booster connects the HTML5 media stream to a Web Audio GainNode and BiquadFilter equalizer to amplify quiet video audio tracks up to 300% without distortion.

Can I load external subtitle files (.SRT / .VTT)?

Yes! You can load any .SRT or .VTT subtitle file, customize font sizing, and adjust subtitle sync timing offsets in real time.

How do I repeat a specific part of a video (A-B Loop)?

Click 'Set Point A' at the start timestamp and 'Set Point B' at the end timestamp (or press [ and ] on your keyboard). The player will continuously repeat that specific segment.

Is my video uploaded to any server?

Zero bytes are uploaded. All video decoding, audio filtering, and snapshot captures run 100% locally in your web browser.
