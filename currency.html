Forex & Currency Converter FAQs

What is the difference between Mid-Market Rate and Bank Rate?

The mid-market rate is the true midpoint between buying and selling rates on global financial markets. Banks and credit cards rarely give you this rate; they add a hidden markup of 1% to 4%. Our tool shows you both the real market rate and your estimated bank fees!
