Scientific Calculator FAQs

What is the difference between DEG and RAD mode?

In Degree (DEG) mode, angles are measured in degrees (0° to 360°), where sin(90) = 1. In Radian (RAD) mode, angles are measured in radians based on π, where sin(π/2) = 1. Use the DEG/RAD toggle to switch easily.
