How does the Cricket Score Counter work?
Our Cricket Scorer is a comprehensive match tracker. It tracks live striker & non-striker stats, overs, bowling figures, extras (wides/no-balls), target chase calculations, celebration sounds, and allows exporting scorecards as PNG images.
